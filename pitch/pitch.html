How it works

  • Replays the log line by line (×600) — never looks ahead
  • Detectors: silence, trends → time-to-failure, safety controls, post-deploy regressions, PII, outages
  • Incident manager: 1 alert, then escalate / resolve updates
  • P1→ pager + phone to clinical shift lead (<1 min) · P2→ on-call (<30 min) · P3/P4 → end-of-day report
  • PII redacted before anything leaves the engine
